THE PLATFORM

From the first notice
to the final review.

One connected workflow for the work you want to win. Here is what happens at each step, and where your team stays in control.

01 / THE WORKFLOW

Discover the right work.

A more useful starting point.

VAGLO monitors SAM.gov on a schedule configured for your firm, filters notices using your NAICS codes and set-aside criteria, and gathers the solicitation package.

Keep the pursuit current.

Tracked notices are checked for amendments, deadline changes, and withdrawals. The system reads the base solicitation and attachments, with amendments taking precedence.

See why it fits.

Opportunities are scored against your firm’s experience, geography, roles, and qualifications. Your own rules can adjust that score, with the reasoning visible.

✳
Your checkpoint

Review the extracted scope and requirements beside their source text. Approve the reading before the proposal is built.

02 / THE WORKFLOW

Build from what you know.

Start with the right experience.

Your portfolio is ranked against the solicitation. Projects that fail a threshold remain visible with a reason. You choose the projects, personnel, and subconsultants.

Turn records into sections.

Generate personnel résumés, project sheets, the participation matrix, and a qualifications narrative. The package is assembled with its cover, contents, dividers, and page numbers.

Keep the team connected.

Partner qualifications and project participation come into the same workflow, so the submission reflects who actually did the work.

✳
Your checkpoint

Review the selections and generated language. The system flags unsupported claims and missing information for your team to resolve.

03 / THE WORKFLOW

Know what still needs you.

Check against the actual ask.

The finished package is checked against the solicitation’s requirements. Findings sit beside the source language, so a reviewer can understand what needs attention.

Make missing facts visible.

Unverified details stay as blanks. Staffing commitments and other facts only your firm can confirm need a human answer before the package is ready.

Review the finished story.

A final pass assesses the package against the published evaluation factors, giving your team another view before making the submission decision.

✳
Your checkpoint

Your firm reviews and approves the package. VAGLO supports the preparation; the decision to submit stays with you.

A GOOD FIT STARTS WITH CLARITY

Purpose-built.
With a clear scope.

Made for federal qualifications.

The proposal workflow produces SF330 packages for federal design work. It does not write general commercial RFP responses.

Your archive matters.

The quality of the package depends on the quality of the records. Setup includes reconciling your project and personnel history with your team.

A private, managed installation.

Your firm has its own database and pipeline. We handle setup and upkeep, and train your point person. The product has been used by one firm with one primary user; simultaneous editing by a large proposal team is not yet proven.
