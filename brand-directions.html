Meet Rhodo ↗

A name with roots.

Rhodo, from rhodonite.

Our name comes from rhodonite, Massachusetts’ state gemstone. It connects the company to its roots—and to the value of knowing what to look for.

Like finding a rare stone among countless others, Rhodo sifts through federal opportunities to surface the ones worth pursuing. Your firm’s experience helps reveal where the real fit is.

The faceted symbol reflects that idea: one valuable find, brought into focus.
