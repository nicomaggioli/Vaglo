A name with roots.

Rhodo, from rhodonite.

Our name comes from rhodonite, Massachusetts’ state gemstone. It connects the company to its roots—and to the value of knowing what to look for.

Like finding a rare stone among countless others, Rhodo sifts through federal opportunities to surface the ones worth pursuing. Your firm’s experience helps reveal where the real fit is.

The faceted symbol reflects that idea: one valuable find, brought into focus.

Why we’re building Rhodo

Good work deserves
a stronger case.

Every project and every person adds to what a firm knows. But when an opportunity arrives, bringing that experience together can mean searching through old files, chasing details, and starting familiar documents again.

We’re building Rhodo to connect those pieces—from finding the right federal work to preparing a proposal that reflects the firm behind it.

AI does the searching, analysis, and first-draft preparation. The people who know the work make the decisions.
