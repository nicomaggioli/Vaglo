Who is VAGLO built for?

Small architecture and engineering firms pursuing federal qualifications-based design work. It is strongest when your firm already has a project archive and wants more proposal capacity. Discovery and analysis also support construction management pursuits.

What does VAGLO actually produce?

An SF330 qualifications package assembled from your firm’s record: personnel résumés, project sheets, a participation matrix, narratives, and partner qualifications. Your team reviews the result before submission.

Does it replace our proposal team?

Your people stay in charge of pursuit decisions, team selection, and final approval. VAGLO handles the repeatable work around those decisions so a small team can spend more time on the pursuit itself.

How does pricing work?

A one-time setup based on the size and condition of your archive, followed by a flat monthly fee for monitoring, maintenance, and included support. You receive the scope and both prices in writing. See how pricing works.

Can we see it with a real pursuit?

Yes. The first conversation is about how your firm works today. If there is a fit, we walk you through a working installation and scope what your setup would involve.
